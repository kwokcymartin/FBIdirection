Magnetic Force Sprint

A light-theme English quiz game for learning the direction of the magnetic force on a current-carrying wire in a magnetic field.

F = BIL sin θ
F⃗ = I L⃗ × B⃗
Use conventional current. The force is perpendicular to both the current and the magnetic field.

Teacher-Friendly Notes single file

  • Practice mode lets students check one question at a time.
  • Timed mode auto-loads the next question after each answer.
  • The red force arrow appears after answering or pressing Reveal Force.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Arrow keys for up/down/left/right, Z for out of page, X for into page.
